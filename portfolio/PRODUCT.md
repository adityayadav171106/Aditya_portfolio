# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and internship providers, university admissions committees, developers and potential collaborators, and people curious about Aditya's work and interests.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Aditya, an engineering student. It introduces him, provides a home for real projects when they are ready to share, and makes it easy to contact him. Success means visitors can quickly understand his interests and find a reliable way to reach him.

## Positioning

The portfolio combines an engineering student's work with his broad, self-described curiosity. It should feel personal rather than like an overstated corporate profile.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- A single-page React and Vite site with About, Work, and Contact content.
- A Work area may be populated later; there are no verified project details to publish yet.
- Current interests supplied by Aditya include coding, chess, cats, robots, and exploring different interests.
- No backend service is currently configured. Do not represent Spotify, AniList, or Duolingo information as live without a supported connection and verified data.
- Existing email and social links in the site are confirmed as accurate.

## Brand Commitments

- Personal, slightly unconventional, intellectually curious, and technically oriented.
- The phrase “just your average nerd ;)” captures the intended voice.
- The supplied `dist/hero_new.png` is the primary visual reference for this redesign.

## Evidence on Hand

- Verified contact links are in `src/components/Contact.jsx`.
- The current About section and requested interests are in the supplied design brief; the interests are confirmed by Aditya.
- The Work section contains placeholders only. No real project descriptions, technologies, demos, or achievements have been supplied.
- The supplied hero collage is `dist/hero_new.png`.

## Product Principles

- Keep personal claims grounded in information Aditya supplied.
- Make the introduction, project archive, and contact path easy to understand.
- Leave empty project details visibly empty instead of presenting invented work as real.
- Treat external activity data as live only when it comes from a supported, configured source.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Preserve keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support.
